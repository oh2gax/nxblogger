Import from WordPress

In WordPress go to Tools → Export → All content and download the XML file. Images are not inside the XML: upload a .zip of wp-content/uploads from your backup, or let the importer download them from the old site while it is still online. Importing again is safe – posts imported earlier are skipped unless you choose to update them.

Big sites (hundreds of images to download) are faster with the command line importer – see README.
